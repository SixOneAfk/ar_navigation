Key logic to port

1) Motion and orientation data hook
File: src/hooks/useGyroscope.ts
- Adds permission flow for DeviceOrientationEvent and DeviceMotionEvent.
- Captures raw acceleration and stores calibrated values in motionRef.
- Exposes:
  - state
  - orientationRef
  - motionRef
  - calibration, motionCalibration, motionCalibrating
  - calibrate(), calibrateMotion(), resetMotionCalibration(), requestPermission(), stop()

2) Camera movement controller
File: src/components/GyroCamera.tsx
- Applies yaw (alpha) to camera rotation each frame.
- Computes forward and strafe vectors from camera orientation.
- Movement modes:
  - gyro: rotation only (tilt movement disabled)
  - buttons: explicit button-based translation
  - walk: step-like forward movement from motion z acceleration
- Walk logic details:
  - reads motion.z
  - low-pass filter (alpha ~ 0.16)
  - thresholding (abs(filteredZ) > 0.12)
  - integrates to walkVelocity with damping
  - computes moveAmount from velocity, dt, sensitivity, walkSpeed
  - applies forward translation with camera.position.addScaledVector(moveDirection, moveAmount)

3) App wiring and controls
File: src/App.tsx
- Integrates useGyroscope + GyroCamera.
- Adds moveMode state: off | gyro | buttons | walk.
- Adds walk controls and calibration UI.
- Passes motionRef, moveMode, walkSpeed, buttonState into GyroCamera.
- Uses onPoseChange for tracking/calibration feedback.

4) Permission UX (camera panel)
File: src/components/CameraPermissionPanel.tsx
- Requests camera and starts video preview.
- Permission panel hides after cameraState becomes granted.
